# MealMonitor — Product Overview

MealMonitor is a mobile app (iOS/Android) that helps users track meals, discover recipes, and build healthy eating habits through gamification.

## Core Features

- **Recipe discovery & cooking** — browse, filter, and cook recipes; completing them earns XP and points
- **Ingredient pantry** — track in-stock ingredients; recipes are matched against what users have available
- **Calorie tracking** — daily calorie log with goal setting and progress tracking
- **Challenges** — time-limited cooking challenges with tasks and reward points
- **Gamification** — XP/leveling system, streaks, badges, and activity feed
- **Onboarding** — guided setup collecting dietary preferences, health goals, and restrictions
- **Personalized recommendations** — recipes scored by ingredient match, time of day, calorie alignment, and dietary preferences

## Target Audience

Health-conscious users who want structured guidance around cooking and nutrition, with a motivation layer built on streaks and achievement systems.

## Brand / Theme

Green-focused UI (`#22c55e`, `#dcfce7`, `#166534`). Light mode only (portrait orientation).
